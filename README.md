## About Me

I turn complex business rules into products people actually enjoy using. In 5+ years I've shipped platforms for startups and growing businesses, from e-commerce and CRMs to FinTech and construction software.

- 🚀 &nbsp;Took a **FinTech payments & escrow platform** from zero to production on my own (Go microservices + React)
- 🎨 &nbsp;Owned the **UI of a construction estimating & project management platform** in a team
- 🧠 &nbsp;Integrating AI and LLMs (**Claude**, **OpenAI**) into real products
- 🔐 &nbsp;Comfortable with the hard parts: RBAC, multi-tenancy, 2FA, payments, observability
- 🌍 &nbsp;Based in **Karlovac, Croatia**, working globally · Fluent in English
